Common questions

The practical details.

Do we need to redesign our machines?+

No. The platform is designed to work with existing controls and common industrial protocols. We start by mapping what your PLC already exposes and add only the minimum edge hardware required.

Does the customer see the rwses brand?+

Not unless you want them to. Customer-facing applications can use your brand, domain, terminology, machine models, and support workflows.

Who owns the machine data?+

Your commercial agreements define ownership. The platform can be configured around your data policies and the boundaries you establish with each customer.

Can we begin with an internal service tool?+

Yes. Many manufacturers start by improving their own remote support, then package selected monitoring features into a paid customer service once the value is proven.
